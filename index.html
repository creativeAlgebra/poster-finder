Poster Finder

Search a movie, click its poster to copy it, then in Subtextual's Library click your document's card and press ⌘V.

Paste your TMDB API key once (themoviedb.org → Settings → API, the "API Read Access Token" or the shorter "API Key"). It stays in this browser.
